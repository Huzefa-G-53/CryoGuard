It solves two critical problems: eliminating 100% of false alarms caused by harmless transients, while providing up to 47 minutes of early warning before real thermal excursions occur.

          ⚡ How to Test the 6 Simulation Scenarios on This Page
        

        

          

            
1. Normal (Baseline Homeostasis)

            
The fridge cycles safely between 4.1°C and 4.9°C. Duty cycle is nominal (38%). Nothing alerts because the medicine is in safe equilibrium.

          

          

            
2. Door Opening (False Alarm Filter Demo)

            
Door opens at T+71m for 3.2m. Air spikes to 6.8°C, but liquid medicine core stays cold at 4.62°C. CryoGuard suppresses the alarm!

          

          

            
3. Coil Defrost (Routine Heater Demo)

            
Heater energizes at T+60m for 15m to melt ice on cooling coils. Air warms, but compressor interlock proves it's benign. Alert filtered!

          

          

            
4. Sensor Glitch (Outlier Rejection)

            
RF transient causes single-sample jump to 12.8°C. An 8°C jump in 60s violates thermal mass laws; Hampel filter rejects the spike.

          

          

            
5. Refrigerant Leak (47m Early Warning Hero)

            
Slow leak starts at T+55m. CryoGuard alerts 47 minutes BEFORE temperature crosses 8.0°C! Dumb systems alert with 0 lead time.

          

          

            
6. Compressor Stall (Critical Emergency)

            
Motor stalls at T+85m (0 RPM). Total cooling cessation. CryoGuard initiates emergency bay transfer protocol immediately.
